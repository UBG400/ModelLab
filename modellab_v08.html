What this version does: it offers 8 hand-built 3D models with written explanations. No paid AI, account or generation is involved: the prompt box only matches keywords to those models and cannot create new ones. Biology links go to OpenStax Biology 2e and the physics link to OpenStax University Physics, both free textbooks.

Drag to rotate · scroll/pinch to zoom · hover to preview a structure's name · click a structure or its label to inspect it · use the buttons in the details panel to go deeper (a greyed-out button names the model to open first) · Ctrl/⌘+Enter finds a model from the prompt.

Read the biology source ↗
Educational illustrations are simplified, not to scale, and are not molecular-level simulations. Only the gas particles and the proton motion are animated, and both are illustrative: gas particles move at random speeds and bounce off the walls without colliding with each other, and no pressure, temperature or energy is calculated. All other scenes are static illustrations. Always check detailed coursework against your class materials and linked references.
